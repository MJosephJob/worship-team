# Member Onboarding Guide

## How to add a new member

1. Log in to the portal as Admin or Super Admin.
2. Go to **Settings** (gear icon in the navigation).
3. Under the **Members** tab, click **Add Member**.
4. Fill in:
   - Full Name
   - Email address
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Gender (important for prayer partner pairing)
   - Instrument / Role
   - Birthday in DD/MM format (e.g., 25/12)
   - Temporary password — give this to the member privately
5. Click **Add Member**.

The member is now in the system. Share the portal URL and their temporary password with them directly (via WhatsApp or in person).

---

## What the new member experiences on first login

### Step 1 — Welcome screen
The member logs in with their email and temporary password. They are immediately redirected to the Onboarding Flow — they cannot access the main portal until onboarding is complete.

### Step 2 — Vision & Mission Reader
The member sees the team's full Vision, Mission, and Values text in an elegant full-screen view. They must scroll all the way to the bottom before the Continue button activates. This ensures they actually read it.

### Step 3 — Quiz
The member answers quiz questions about the V&M content, one question at a time. They must score 100% to proceed.

If they get any wrong, they see which questions they missed, and they're asked to go back and re-read before trying again. There are unlimited attempts — the goal is comprehension, not gatekeeping.

### Step 4 — Typed Confirmation
The member types a specific phrase exactly to confirm their commitment. Characters turn gold as they match the phrase in real time.

### Step 5 — Onboarding Checklist
The member sees a checklist of onboarding tasks (e.g., "Attend first rehearsal", "Meet with worship leader"). They can check items off as they complete them. When all required items are checked, a celebration animation plays and they're welcomed to the team.

You (the Admin) receive an in-app notification and email when any member completes onboarding.

---

## How to track onboarding progress

1. Go to **Settings → Members**.
2. Each member card shows an **Onboarded** (green) or **Pending** (amber) badge.
3. You can see the onboarding status at a glance for all members.

On the **Dashboard**, the Admin section shows an "Onboarded %" stat card, so you always know what percentage of the team has completed onboarding.

---

## What if a member gets stuck or needs to reset?

1. Go to **Settings → Members**.
2. Find the member and use the role/status controls to reset if needed.
3. Alternatively, contact the member directly and walk them through it.

If a member forgets their password, they can use the **Forgot Password** link on the login screen to receive a reset email (requires EmailJS to be configured).

---

## After onboarding

Once a member completes onboarding:
- They can access the full portal
- They receive the **Onboarded** badge on their profile
- They will be included in prayer partner pairing
- They start receiving monthly V&M review reminders
- Their attendance starts being tracked
